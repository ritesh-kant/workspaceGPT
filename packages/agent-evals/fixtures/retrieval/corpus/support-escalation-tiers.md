# Support Escalation Tiers

Tier 1 handles account and billing questions with a 24-hour SLA. Tier 2
handles technical issues requiring engineering context, 8-hour SLA. Tier 3
is a direct escalation to the on-call engineer, reserved for outages
affecting more than 10% of customers.
